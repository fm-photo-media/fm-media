# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 28, 2026

This policy explains how FM Media ("we", "us"), a real estate photography business owned by Francisco Martinez and serving Chicagoland, handles personal information collected through https://fm-media.org (the "Site").

## 1. What we collect

**When you send a shoot request** through our booking form, we collect:

- your name, email address and phone number
- your realtor or agency name, if you give it
- the property address, and its approximate square footage if you give it
- the services you pick (photo package, add-ons, video) and whether you're claiming our first-shoot offer
- your preferred date, and a preferred time window or start time if you give one
- your message

We also record when your request arrived and whether our notification email about it was sent.

**When you call, text or email us.** If you use the Site's call, text or email links, your message goes straight to our phone or inbox, not through the Site. We use what you send, such as the property address and the shoot you want, in the same way as a booking request.

**Spam protection.** When someone sends the booking form or tries to sign in to our admin area, the Site stores a scrambled code made from the connection's IP address (a keyed, one-way hash), not the IP address itself, together with the time. We use it only to limit how many requests one connection can send. Old entries are deleted automatically.

**Automatically, through our host.** Like most websites, our hosting provider processes technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is used to deliver and secure the Site, and the host keeps it only briefly under its own policies.

**What we don't do.** The Site has no analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or social media trackers, and it doesn't collect payment information. It sets no cookies for visitors. The only cookie is a sign-in cookie for our own admin area, which ends after 12 hours or when we sign out.

## 2. How we use it

We use your information only to:

- reply to your request, check availability, and schedule and deliver your shoot
- contact you about your request or shoot by phone, text or email
- check eligibility for the first-shoot offer, if you claim it
- keep a record of the requests we receive
- protect the Site from spam and abuse

We don't use your booking details for marketing, and we don't sell them.

## 3. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Site, and only as far as they need it:

- **Vercel Inc.** hosts the Site and stores our portfolio images.
- **Our database hosting provider** stores booking requests.
- **Resend** delivers a notification email about each booking request to our inbox. Resend keeps a record of each message for up to 30 days.
- **Our email provider** holds the inbox where those notifications arrive and from which we reply to you.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety. If the business is sold or transferred, booking records may go with it.

## 4. Property photos in our portfolio

Photos in our online portfolio are public. How we may use photos from a shoot is described in our [Terms of Service](/terms). If you'd like photos of a property removed from our portfolio, contact us and we'll delete them from the Site and from our image storage.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep booking requests in our booking system for up to 24 months after we receive them, then delete them.

A copy of each request also arrives in our email inbox, and that copy stays there until we delete it. Texts and emails you send us directly stay on our phone or in our inbox until we delete them. If you ask us to delete your information, we delete those copies too. Our email-sending provider keeps its own record of each notification for up to 30 days.

Invoices and records of completed shoots, which we keep outside this Site, may be kept longer where tax or accounting rules require.

## 6. Your choices and requests

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you
- correct it
- delete it

Email femartinez211@gmail.com or call or text (312) 375-5209. We may need to confirm it's really you before we act on a request. We won't treat you differently for making one.

## 7. Security

Booking requests can be viewed only through our password-protected admin area, which limits sign-in attempts. Access is limited to FM Media and the people who maintain the Site for us. No way of storing or sending data is completely secure, but we limit who can see your information and keep it only as long as described above.

## 8. Biometric information

We don't collect biometric information (such as face geometry), and we don't use face recognition on any photos.

## 9. Children

The Site is for real estate professionals, property owners and hosts. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## 10. Do Not Track

The Site doesn't track visitors across other websites, so browser "Do Not Track" signals don't change how it works.

## 11. California residents

California law (CalOPPA) asks us to describe the kinds of personal information we collect and who we share it with. Those are listed in sections 1 and 3. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top. If we make a significant change to how we use booking information, we'll also post a notice on the Site.
